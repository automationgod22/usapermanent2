# IRIS campaign imagery

Four original fictional beauty campaign images generated with the built-in image generation tool for this demo. These do not depict real IRIS clients, treatment results, the practitioner, or business premises. Attribution is displayed on the website. No before-and-after claim or client endorsement is implied.

Final website files: `iris-portrait.webp`, `iris-brows.webp`, `iris-lips.webp`, `iris-still-life.webp`. The WebP versions retain the generated compositions and are compressed for local delivery; original generated images remain in the generation library.

Cream plaster backdrop, deep espresso shadows, understated champagne highlights, no jewelry except a small simple gold stud, bare shoulders tastefully cropped. A high-end beauty editorial shot on medium-format film, beautifully subtle, intimate and tactile. Face fills the upper center so this works in a rounded vertical website image frame. No text, no logos, no watermark, no objects, no before-and-after, no treatment in progress, no visible tattoos, no claims of medical results. This is an AI campaign concept, not a real client.
